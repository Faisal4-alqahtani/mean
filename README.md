# Ma’an Platform (منصة معاً) 🚗

> A unified digital platform designed to streamline vehicle-related reporting, reducing misinformation and enhancing public awareness through secure and verified reporting mechanisms.

---

## 💡 The Problem
Inundating social media with unverified vehicle reports leads to information clutter, fake alerts, and resource exhaustion for authorities and owners. **Ma’an** solves this by providing a dedicated, secure channel that ensures data integrity and prevents duplicate reporting.

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Verified Reporting:** Multi-step verification to ensure the authenticity of every report.
- **Anti-Duplicate System:** A smart filtering mechanism to prevent multiple reports for the same incident, centralizing the data.
- **Anti-Fake Views:** Algorithms to detect and filter out non-organic engagement and bot activity.
- **Awareness Engine:** Built-in modules to educate users on safe reporting practices and digital security.

## 🧠 Technical Challenge: The "Vanishing Vehicle" Logic
**The Challenge:** How to track and locate a vehicle before it disappears from its last recorded location?
**The Solution:** I integrated a custom **Predictive Analysis Logic** using PHP and historical data patterns via API integration. This feature estimates the potential movement radius of a vehicle based on the time elapsed and last known location, allowing for faster response times before the vehicle leaves the area.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, **Bootstrap 5** (Responsive & Mobile-First).
- **Backend:** **PHP** (Core Logic & Server-side management).
- **Database:** **MySQL** (Structured Data Management for Reports & Users).
- **APIs:** Integration of Location Services and Verification APIs.
